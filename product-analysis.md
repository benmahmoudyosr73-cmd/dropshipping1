## Product-by-Product Analysis

### Product 2: 4PCS Chef Knife Set

**Strengths:**
- Kitchen/cooking niche is evergreen and massive on social media
- Knife sets have strong perceived value — people expect to pay $50+
- Great for visual content (cutting demos, unboxing, satisfying slicing videos)
- Higher ticket = better margin per sale

**Risks:**
- Blade types/steel unconfirmed — critical for credibility
- Shipping damage risk with blades
- Competitive niche with established brands
- Need to confirm exact specs before running ads

**Verdict:** Best margin opportunity of the three. Worth pursuing once CJ confirms specs.

### Product 3: Magnetic Cable Clips

**Strengths:**

## Marketing Campaign Strategy

### Channel Priority

1. **TikTok (organic + ads)** — highest ROI for product discovery
2. **YouTube Shorts** — repurpose TikTok content, longer shelf life
3. **Instagram Reels** — third channel, same content repurposed
4. **Facebook/Meta Ads** — retargeting warm audiences only

### Content Strategy by Product

#### Knife Set — "Satisfying Cuts" Content

## Ad Strategy & Budget

### Phase 1: Validation (Week 1–2) — $150–300 total

- **Platform:** TikTok Ads (Spark Ads)
- **Approach:** Post 3–5 organic videos per product. Boost the one that gets the best organic traction as a Spark Ad.
- **Budget:** $15–20/day on the winning creative
- **Target:** Broad targeting, let TikTok's algorithm find buyers
- **Goal:** Find a winning creative with <$15 CPA (cost per acquisition)

### Phase 2: Scale (Week 3–4) — $500–1000

- **Scale winning ad sets** to $50–100/day
- **Launch retargeting** on Meta (Facebook/Instagram) for site visitors who didn't buy
- **Create 3 new creatives** based on what worked in Phase 1
- **Add YouTube Shorts** ads if TikTok proves the concept

### Phase 3: Diversify (Month 2+)

- Build a branded Shopify store around the winning product category
- Bundle products (knife set + cutting board, cable clips + desk mat)
- Launch email flows for repeat purchases
- Test influencer seeding (send free product to micro-creators, 5K–50K followers)

## AI Video Strategy

### Tools to Use

| Tool | Use Case | Cost |
|------|----------|------|
| **CapCut** (free) | Edit TikTok/Shorts, add text overlays, trending sounds | Free |
| **HeyGen** | AI spokesperson for product reviews/demos | ~$24/mo |
| **ElevenLabs** | AI voiceover for product showcases | ~$5/mo |
| **Canva AI** | Thumbnails, ad creatives, social posts | ~$13/mo |
| **Runway ML** | AI B-roll generation (lifestyle scenes) | ~$12/mo |

### AI Video Workflow

1. **Film real product footage** (phone camera is fine — authenticity sells)
2. **Add AI voiceover** (ElevenLabs) for professional narration
3. **Generate lifestyle B-roll** (Runway) to fill gaps
4. **Edit in CapCut** with trending hooks, text overlays, and music
5. **Create AI spokesperson version** (HeyGen) as an alternative creative to test

### Content Calendar

- Post **1–2 videos/day** across TikTok + YouTube Shorts
- Rotate between product demos, before/after, unboxing, and "hack" formats
- Repurpose every TikTok to YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels (same content, 3x reach)

## 3. AI Video Production Pipeline

**Monthly tooling budget by tier:**
- **Starter (5 videos/week):** CapCut Free + ElevenLabs = **$5/mo**
- **Growth (15 videos/week):** + HeyGen + Canva = **$42/mo**
- **Scale (30 videos/week):** + Runway + Kling + OpusClip = **$76/mo**

### Step-by-Step Workflows

#### Workflow 1: AI Voiceover Product Showcase (easiest, do this first)

1. **Film product footage** on your phone (natural light, clean background, 60fps for slow-mo)
2. **Write the script** — use one of the 30 scripts above
3. **Generate voiceover** in ElevenLabs:
   - Pick a voice that matches your brand (warm male, energetic female, etc.)
   - Paste script → adjust pacing with commas and periods
   - Download MP3
4. **Edit in CapCut:**
   - Import footage + voiceover
   - Sync cuts to the voiceover beats
   - Add text overlays for key claims ("4 knives, $55", "each piece is unique")
   - Add auto-captions (CapCut does this automatically)
   - Add trending sound underneath at 10–15% volume
5. **Export** at 1080x1920 (9:16 vertical) → post to TikTok, Shorts, Reels

**Time per video:** 45 min–1 hour

#### Workflow 2: AI Avatar Product Review (medium effort, high trust)

1. **Create your avatar** in HeyGen:
   - Use a stock avatar or upload a photo to create a custom one
   - Pick a consistent look — this becomes your "brand host" across all videos
   - Choose voice and language
2. **Write review script** (use Script K9, C9, or P9 as templates)
3. **Generate the video** in HeyGen — avatar delivers the review naturally
4. **Edit in CapCut:**
   - Cut in real product footage as B-roll while avatar talks
   - Add text overlays and captions
   - Add product close-ups when avatar mentions specific features
5. **Export and post**

**Time per video:** 30 min (mostly waiting for HeyGen to render)

#### Workflow 3: AI-Generated Lifestyle Scenes (advanced, highest production value)

1. **Write scene prompts** for Runway/Kling:
   - Knife set: "Professional chef knife set on white marble countertop, morning light streaming through window, herbs and vegetables arranged nearby, cinematic 4K"
   - Phone stand: "Resin and wood phone stand on minimalist Scandinavian desk, warm afternoon light, shallow depth of field, aesthetic workspace"
   - Cable clips: "Clean organized desk setup with magnetic cable clips holding white cables against dark desk edge, overhead shot, soft lighting"
2. **Generate 5-second clips** — run 3–4 variations per prompt, keep the best
3. **Combine with real footage** in CapCut — AI scenes for the "hero" beauty shots, real footage for the functional demos
4. **Add AI voiceover** from ElevenLabs
5. **Final edit** with transitions, text, and music

**Time per video:** 1–1.5 hours (generation time + editing)

#### Workflow 4: Batch Content Machine (scale mode)

1. **Film one 30-minute session** per product — capture every angle, demo, unboxing
2. **In CapCut, create a "master" project** with all footage organized by shot type
3. **Generate 5 different edits** from the same footage:
   - Version A: Fast-paced with trending audio (15s)
   - Version B: ASMR cut with raw sounds (30s)
   - Version C: Voiceover story version (45s)
   - Version D: Comparison/review cut (60s)
   - Version E: AI avatar introduction + B-roll (30s)
4. **Post each version on a different day** — same footage, 5x the content

**One filming session → 5 videos → 15 posts (TikTok + Shorts + Reels)**

## 4. Platform Playbooks

### TikTok Playbook

**Account Warmup (Days 1–7, before posting any product content):**
1. Day 1–3: Follow 20–30 accounts in your niche (cooking, desk setups, gadgets). Like and comment on 15–20 videos/day. Watch videos fully — don't skip.
2. Day 4–5: Post 2 non-promotional videos (a trending sound, a relatable meme about cooking/WFH). Engage with every comment.
3. Day 6–7: Post your first soft product video (satisfying/ASMR style, no hard sell). Check analytics.

**Hashtag Clusters:**
- Knife Set: #kitchenhacks #cookingtiktok #knifeskills #satisfyingcooking #chefsoftiktok #kitchenupgrade #foodprep #asmrcooking
- Cable Clips: #desksetup #deskorganization #wfhlife #techgadgets #officesetup #cleandesk #organizedhome #gamingsetup
- Phone Stand: #deskdecor #uniquegifts #resinart #aestheticdesk #giftideas #homedecor #phoneholder #coolfinds

**Best Posting Times (US audience):**
- Tue–Thu: 10am, 2pm, 7pm EST
- Fri–Sat: 11am, 4pm, 9pm EST
- Sunday: 10am, 3pm EST
- Post 1–2x daily. Consistency > perfection.

**Spark Ads Setup:**
1. Post organically first — let TikTok's algorithm test the content free
2. After 24–48 hours, check which video has the best completion rate (not just views)
3. Go to TikTok Ads Manager → Create Spark Ad → Select the organic post
4. Set objective: "Website Conversions" with your Shopify pixel
5. Budget: $20/day, broad targeting (age 18–55, US), let TikTok optimize
6. Run for 3 days → evaluate CPA → kill if CPA > 2x your margin

**TikTok Shop (if eligible):**
- Apply at seller-us.tiktok.com
- Zero ad spend needed — products appear in your videos and on your profile
- TikTok takes ~5% commission but drives organic sales
- Pair with affiliate program — creators promote your product for a commission

### YouTube Shorts Playbook

**Why Shorts Matter:**
- Shorts have a much longer viral shelf life — a TikTok dies in 48 hours, a Short can get recommended for months
- YouTube's audience skews slightly older and higher income — better for $50+ products
- Shorts feed directly into long-form — a viral Short can drive subscribers who watch your reviews

**Shorts-to-Long-Form Funnel:**
1. Post Shorts daily (repurpose TikToks — just remove watermark with SnapTik)
2. Once a Short hits 50K+ views, create a full 5–8 min review of that product
3. The long-form video gets the affiliate/product link in the description
4. Pin a comment: "Full review on my channel!" on the Short

**SEO Title Templates:**
- "Best Kitchen Knife Set Under $60 — Honest Review"
- "I Tested the Viral Cable Clips from TikTok — Are They Worth It?"
- "This Resin Phone Stand Is a Work of Art (Unboxing + Review)"

**YouTube Shopping Tags:**
- Enable YouTube Shopping in YouTube Studio → Monetization
- Tag your products directly in Shorts and long-form videos
- Viewers can tap to buy without leaving YouTube

### Instagram Reels Playbook

**Algorithm Differences from TikTok:**
- Instagram favors saved and shared content over just views
- Carousel posts get 2x the reach of single images — pair with Reels
- Instagram audiences respond to polished, aesthetic content more than raw UGC

**Combo Strategy:**
1. Post a Reel (repurposed TikTok) showing the product in action
2. Same day, post a Carousel: "5 Reasons This Knife Set is Worth $55" — educational, save-worthy
3. The Carousel drives saves (algorithm boost), the Reel drives reach
4. Stories: poll stickers ("Would you buy this?"), countdown to restock, behind-the-scenes

**Instagram Shop:**
- Set up through Meta Commerce Manager
- Tag products directly in Reels and posts
- Creates a shoppable profile tab

## 5. Paid Ads Masterplan

### Testing Framework

**Phase 1: Creative Testing (Week 1–2, $150–300)**
- Structure: 1 Campaign → 1 Ad Set → 3–5 Ads (different creatives)
- Use CBO (Campaign Budget Optimization) at $20/day
- Each ad = different hook/angle from the scripts above
- Let run for 3 full days before making decisions

**Kill Rules:**
- Kill an ad if: CPA > 2x your target after 3 days AND 1000+ impressions
- Kill the entire ad set if: no purchases after $50 spend
- Never kill before 3 days — the algorithm needs learning time

**Scale Rules:**
- If an ad has CPA below target → increase budget 20% every 2 days (not more, or you reset the algorithm)
- If 2+ ads are profitable → duplicate the ad set with different audiences
- Never increase budget more than 20% in a single day

### Creative Testing Matrix

From just 3 filming sessions, you can create 18 ad variations:

| | Hook A: Problem | Hook B: Unboxing | Hook C: Comparison |
|---|---|---|---|
| Body 1: Demo | A1 | B1 | C1 |
| Body 2: Story | A2 | B2 | C2 |
| Body 3: ASMR | A3 | B3 | C3 |
| x CTA 1: "Link in bio" | 9 versions | | |
| x CTA 2: "Shop now" | 9 versions | | |
| **Total** | **18 unique ads** | | |

Test 5 at a time. Kill losers, scale winners, rotate in new ones weekly.

### Retargeting Ladder

Once your pixel has data (after ~50 purchases), build this funnel:

### Budget Calculator

```

Daily revenue = Daily sales x Sell price
Daily profit = Daily sales x ($Z - $Y)

3.3 x $59.99 = $198/day revenue
3.3 x ($29 - $15) = $46/day profit = ~$1,400/month profit
```

## 7. Monetization Beyond Direct Sales

### Revenue Stream 1: TikTok Creativity Program
- Requires 10K followers + 100K views in 30 days
- Pays $0.50–$1.00 per 1,000 qualified views (videos must be 60s+)

- Once you hit 1,000 subscribers + 4,000 watch hours (or 10M Shorts views in 90 days)
- Long-form reviews earn $3–$8 per 1,000 views (CPM varies by niche)

- A single viral review video can earn $500–$2,000 in ad revenue

### Revenue Stream 3: Affiliate Marketing
- Sign up for Amazon Associates, CJ Affiliate, ShareASale
- Review competing products on YouTube → earn 3–8% commission on every sale
- "Best knife sets under $100" roundup video → your product #1, competitors #2–5 with affiliate links

- Once you're doing 100+ orders/month on the knife set, approach CJ about private labeling
- Custom logo on the knives, branded packaging, insert cards

- If you create viral product videos, other dropshippers will want to use your content

- Your filming skills become a sellable asset independent of the products

### Go / No-Go Decision Tree

```
Week 2 checkpoint:
  Has any creative achieved CPA < margin?
    YES → Scale that creative 20%/day, test 3 new variations
    NO → Have you tested 5+ creatives?
      YES → Pivot: try different product, different angle, or different platform
      NO → Keep testing — you haven't found the winner yet

Week 4 checkpoint:
  Is the campaign profitable after ad spend?
    YES → Double down. Increase budget, add retargeting, launch on second platform
    NO → Is one product clearly outperforming?
      YES → Kill the others, go all-in on the winner
      NO → Reassess products. Consider testing completely different items.

Week 8 checkpoint:
  Monthly profit > $1,000?
    YES → You have a business. Add email/SMS, private label, second product
    NO but trending up → Keep optimizing. The flywheel is building.
    NO and flat → Time to pivot products or niches entirely.
```
